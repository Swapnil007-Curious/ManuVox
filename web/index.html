                            ManuVox

                            Your hands, spoken aloud.

                            Recognises 250 isolated ASL signs and builds simple sentences. Not a full ASL translator.

                            • Video never leaves your device. Only recognised words are sent for sentence translation; without a connection, sentences are joined on the device and marked “basic”.
                            • Each sign is recognised on its own, from a fixed vocabulary of 250 signs. Grammar from facial expressions, space and movement is not captured, so a sentence is a rough English reading of the glosses.
                            • Spoken sentences use your device's own voices.
